Methods

How the lab thinks.

When every model can produce a fluent answer, the scarce thing is method: how questions are framed, how uncertainty is structured, and how disagreement is kept honest.

01

Scenario discipline

Turn uncertainty into structured paths: drivers, shocks, transmission channels, adaptive responses, early indicators and decision points. Never one story; always a distribution.

02

Structured disagreement

Let different models, schools of thought and adversarial roles challenge each other before any synthesis, and keep the minority report.

03

Thought experiments

Test ideas we cannot yet measure through counterfactuals, constraints and moral trade-offs: what changes if one assumption is reversed?

04

Long-horizon reasoning

Reason about institutions, technology absorption and social resilience over decades, where most of the consequences of AI will play out.

What we are building.

Ideas at different stages. Each starts as a method, becomes a tool, and is published openly.

Concept

Scenario Atlas

Run the published scenarios. Each major AI scenario (AI 2027, the 2028 crisis, AI 2040, the gradual-disempowerment story) translated into a MoralWorld setup, so anyone can run them side by side, change one assumption, and see which conclusions survive.

In development

Human Agency Monitor

A companion to CARI. CARI measures AI. This measures us: how much human work, decision-making and attention is being handed to AI systems, country by country. The gradual-disempowerment question, made measurable.

In development

Thought Experiment Studio

The human question, debated. Questions with no data yet (a world where no one needs to work, AI companions, machine rights, what remains distinctively human) explored through roles, counterfactuals and multi-model debate, with the disagreements kept on the record.

In MoralWorld desktop

Organisation Lab

AI inside a single institution. How AI changes one firm, bank, hospital or ministry: who adapts, who resists, who stays silent, and how that adds up to what happens to a country.
